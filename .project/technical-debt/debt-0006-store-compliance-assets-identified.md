## Evidence

The repository does not currently contain or document the publication assets and declarations needed for the Play Store release process, including privacy policy, Data Safety answers, store icon/feature graphic preparation, screenshots, and store descriptions.

## Affected components

- Google Play listing
- Privacy/compliance documentation
- Release assets

## Impact

Even with a valid release bundle, the application cannot complete Play Store submission until the required declarations and listing assets are supplied and kept consistent with application behaviour.

## Expected remediation outcome

Prepare the complete Play Store publishing asset set and document truthful privacy/Data Safety declarations based on the app's actual data handling.

## Resolution criteria

- Privacy policy is published and linked from the Play Console listing.
- Data Safety declaration is completed and reviewed against actual app behaviour.
- Store icon, feature graphic, and required screenshots are available in accepted dimensions/formats.
- Short and full descriptions are prepared.
- Release documentation identifies where these assets and declarations are maintained.
